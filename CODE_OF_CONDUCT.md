# Code of Conduct

LibraCore contributors, maintainers, and community members are expected to keep project spaces respectful, constructive, and focused on improving the software.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate with patience and professional respect.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome contributors with different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Give actionable technical feedback and assume good faith unless evidence shows otherwise.
- Respect privacy and never publish another person's private information.
- Follow repository security and responsible-disclosure rules.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory or degrading comments, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, sustained disruption, or knowingly unsafe/security-abusive behavior are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to **supportramsandesh@gmail.com**. Maintainers may edit/remove comments, close or lock discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community and project.

Reports should include enough context to investigate while avoiding unnecessary disclosure of sensitive information. Good-faith reports will be handled as privately as practical.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, project communication, and situations where someone is representing the LibraCore project.

This project favors restoration and clear boundaries when appropriate, while serious or repeated abuse may require immediate restriction.
